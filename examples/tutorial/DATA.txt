car_evaluation.csv
==================

The Car Evaluation data of the UCI Machine Learning Repository
(https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/19/car+evaluation), distributed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).

It holds the 1728 combinations of six attributes of a car and the evaluation
that a hierarchical decision model built with experts gives to each one:

    Bohanec, M. and Rajkovic, V. (1988). Knowledge acquisition and explanation
    for multi-attribute decision making. 8th International Workshop on Expert
    Systems and their Applications, Avignon, France, 59-78.

The file was obtained from the copy of the Penn Machine Learning Benchmarks
(https://github.com/EpistasisLab/pmlb, dataset "car_evaluation"), whose integer
codes were replaced by the labels documented there: the attribute names and
the values are spelled out ("very high" for "vhigh", "5 or more" for "5more",
"unacceptable" for "unacc", and so on). The rows and their order are those of
the original file.
