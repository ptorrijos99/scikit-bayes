Tree Augmented Naive Bayes
--------------------------

A single Bayesian network in which every attribute depends on the class and
on at most one other attribute: how the tree is learned and what it captures.

The dependencies form a tree, learned from the data with the Chow-Liu procedure: the conditional mutual information $I(A_i; A_j \mid C)$ is computed for every pair of attributes, and the tree of maximum total weight is kept.">
