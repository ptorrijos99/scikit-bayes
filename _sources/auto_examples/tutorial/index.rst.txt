Tutorial
--------

One problem on real data from the table to the final evaluation: the Car
Evaluation data, a pandas DataFrame of labels fitted without an encoder, a
comparison with scikit-learn baselines, a grid search, the learned structure
against the known one, the weights, and the test set.
