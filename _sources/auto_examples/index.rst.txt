Tree Augmented Naive Bayes
--------------------------

A single Bayesian network in which every attribute depends on the class and
on at most one other attribute: how the tree is learned and what it captures.



.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbnails">

.. thumbnail-parent-div-open

.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="skbn.TAN (tree augmented naive Bayes) lets every attribute depend on the class and on at most one other attribute. The dependencies form a tree, learned from the data with the Chow-Liu procedure: the conditional mutual information $I(A_i; A_j \mid C)$ is computed for every pair of attributes, and the tree of maximum total weight is kept.">

K-Dependence Bayesian Classifier
--------------------------------

A single Bayesian network in which every attribute depends on the class and
on at most k other attributes: how k trades expressiveness for data.



.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbnails">

.. thumbnail-parent-div-open

.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="skbn.KDB (k-dependence Bayesian classifier) lets every attribute depend on the class and on at most k other attributes. The attributes are added to the network in decreasing order of their mutual information with the class $I(A_i; C)$, and each one takes as parents the min(k, t) attributes, among the t already in the network, with the highest conditional mutual information $I(A_i; A_j \mid C)$.">

.. only:: html

  .. image:: /auto_examples/kdb/images/thumb/sphx_glr_18_plot_kdb_k_dependence_thumb.png
