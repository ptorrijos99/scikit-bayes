K-Dependence Bayesian Classifier
--------------------------------

A single Bayesian network in which every attribute depends on the class and
on at most k other attributes: how k trades expressiveness for data.

.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="skbn.KDB (K-Dependence Bayesian classifier) lets every attribute depend on the class and on at most k other attributes. The attributes are added to the network in decreasing order of their mutual information with the class $I(A_i; C)$, and each one takes as parents the min(k, t) attributes, among the t already in the network, with the highest conditional mutual information $I(A_i; A_j \mid C)$.">
